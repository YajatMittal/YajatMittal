### 🔭 Things I've built

- **[Anything Kart](https://github.com/YajatMittal/anything-kart)**: a browser-based kart racing game where whatever you're holding becomes your steering wheel. Turn a banana like a wheel, flick it to fire items. Hand tracking runs fully on-device. [Play it live →](https://anything-kart.vercel.app)
- **[Tabula](https://github.com/YajatMittal/Tabula)**: snap a photo of a breadboard circuit and get back a KiCad schematic and a routed PCB in under 2 minutes, using OpenCV, a custom Roboflow detector and Gemini. *EurekaHacks 2026*
- **[Memoria](https://github.com/YajatMittal/Memoria)**: an AI memory companion for people living with Alzheimer's. Ask *"What did I do yesterday?"* and get a gentle summary built from your own photos. *CNLC 2024*
- **[SnackTrack](https://github.com/YajatMittal/SnackTrack)**: watches your webcam, sees what you bite into, and scores it. Apple, gain points. Cookie, lose them. Written up as a [tutorial on the Roboflow blog](https://roboflow.com/blog/build-real-time-snack-detection-app).
